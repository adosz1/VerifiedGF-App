## 📄 CONTRACT #1 (ENGLISH TRANSLATION): Restaurant Showcase, Video Release & Liability Waiver

### **COELISAFE — RESTAURANT SHOWCASE, VIDEO RELEASE & LIABILITY INDEMNITY AGREEMENT**

1. **Nature of Service (Informational & Visual Marketing Directory Only):**
   The Restaurant acknowledges that CoeliSafe (`coelisafe.com`) is an independent informational directory and visual marketing platform, **not a government food-safety regulator (such as Hungary’s NÉBIH or the UK FSA), clinical laboratory, or medical certifier**. Any kitchen video or protocol badge reflects solely a point-in-time visual walkthrough demonstrated by the Restaurant on the date of filming.
2. **100% Restaurant Food Safety Responsibility & Indemnification:**
   Under **EU Regulation No. 1169/2011**, Hungarian NÉBIH regulations, and applicable food safety laws, the Restaurant retains **100% sole and exclusive legal responsibility** for all food preparation, ingredient sourcing, staff allergen training, and cross-contamination prevention. The Restaurant agrees to **indemnify, defend, and hold harmless CoeliSafe, its founders, and its local representatives** from any and all claims, liabilities, or damages arising out of any food or beverage served by the Restaurant.
3. **Kitchen Video Filming & Media Release:**
   The Restaurant grants CoeliSafe permission to film a kitchen walkthrough video on-site (or use a video provided by the Restaurant) and publish it on `coelisafe.com` and associated promotional channels.
4. **30-Day Free Trial & No Lock-In:**
   The initial 30-day Founding Partner showcase and video shoot are **100% free with zero obligation**. After 30 days, the Restaurant may opt into the monthly subscription (`14 900 Ft / month`, cancelable anytime) or request immediate removal via `coelisafe@gmail.com`.
